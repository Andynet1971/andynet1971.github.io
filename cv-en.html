Research projects

Football betting backtesting engine — Python

38,225 matches, real odds from ~15 bookmakers, Elo and online Dixon-Coles ratings, 19 strategies (2 of them controls), 291,248 simulated bets. Train / validation / holdout looked at exactly once, an automated leakage test, Benjamini-Hochberg correction for multiple comparisons. The result is negative and I wrote it down: no strategy survives the controls, and the three that win most often — up to 90.7% of bets — all lose money.
